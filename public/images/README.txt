To display your photograph on the portfolio:
1. Place your photo here (e.g., `profile.jpg` or `profile.png`).
2. Open `src/data/profile.ts` and set:
   `profileImageUrl: "/images/profile.jpg"`
3. Save the file. Your portfolio will immediately update!
